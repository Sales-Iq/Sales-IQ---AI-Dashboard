1
Introduction

SalesIQ is an enterprise-grade retail intelligence platform that empowers shop owners with real-time multi-device POS billing, automated FEFO perishable batch tracking, and conversational AI business intelligence powered by Google Gemini to eliminate stockouts and dead capital.

2
Problem Statement

Traditional retail management suffers from unpredicted stockouts, capital locked in stagnant dead stock, and severe financial losses caused by expired perishable goods due to manual inventory logs and lack of real-time synchronization across billing counters.

⚠️
Manual paper journals and static spreadsheets cause up to 24% revenue leakage through inventory spoilage and stock depletion.
